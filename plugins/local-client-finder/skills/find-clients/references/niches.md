# Niches to try

These are trades where many businesses run on word of mouth and a Google listing, so they're good places to LOOK.
Always check the real count with Step 1 before committing; it changes city by city.

Two real searches, for scale (Google Maps, Oct 2026):
- "pressure washing in Tulsa OK": 72 listings, 10 without a Website button (some were car washes, filtered out in Step 1).
- "roofing contractor in Hamilton ON": 34 listings, 3 without a Website button.
Roofing is crowded with websites; smaller service trades usually have more gaps.

Service trades to start with:
- pressure / soft washing, window cleaning, gutter cleaning
- lawn care, landscaping, tree service, snow removal
- house cleaning, carpet cleaning, junk removal, moving
- handyman, painting, fencing, decks, concrete, drywall
- mobile mechanic, mobile detailing, small engine repair
- pool service, pest control, chimney sweep
- barbers, nail and lash studios, tailors, shoe repair
- food trucks, caterers, bakeries working from home kitchens (check local rules)

Tips:
- Suburbs and smaller towns next to a big city often have more businesses with no site than downtown.
- Businesses with lots of reviews and no site are the best leads: busy, trusted, and missing the obvious.
