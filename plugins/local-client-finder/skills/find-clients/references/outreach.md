# Outreach templates

Fill every bracket with the business's REAL public listing data. If you don't have a detail, cut the line; never
make one up. Keep the tone of a neighbour who noticed something, not a sales script.

## The opener (works in every channel)
> "Hi, is this [Business]? I was looking for [service] in [city] and found you on Google: [rating] stars from
> [reviews] reviews, which is great. I noticed you don't have a website, so when people search your name they end up
> on [Yelp / Facebook / other directory] instead. I build simple sites for local businesses. I actually put together a
> quick preview for you. Can I send you the link?"

Why it works: it starts with something true and flattering (their reviews), names a real problem (where customers
land today), and asks for a tiny yes (can I send a link).

## Phone (best reply rate for trades)
- Call during slow hours (mid-morning, mid-afternoon). Not first thing, not during lunch.
- Say the opener. Then stop and listen.
- If yes: "What's the best number or email for the link?" Send it while you're still on the phone.
- If "how much?": "Depends on what you need. Have a look at the preview first, and if you like it we'll talk numbers."
- If no: "No problem, thanks for your time." Mark them done.

## Voicemail (20 seconds)
> "Hi, this is [Name]. I found [Business] on Google, [reviews] reviews, nice work. You don't have a website yet, so I
> made you a free preview of one. If you'd like to see it, call or text me at [number]. Thanks!"

## Walk-in (shops, salons, restaurants, auto)
Bring the preview open on your phone. Ask for the owner or manager, say the opener, hand them the phone.

## Email (only if a business email is on the listing or their Facebook page)
Subject: Website preview for [Business]
> Hi [Business] team,
>
> I came across you on Google Maps: [rating] stars from [reviews] reviews. Customers clearly like you, but there's no
> website for them to land on, so searches go to [directory] instead.
>
> I made a preview of a site for you, built from your Google listing: [preview link]
>
> If you like it, I can set it up on your own domain. If not, no worries at all.
>
> [Name] · [phone]
>
> (Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.)

## Facebook / Instagram DM (for FACEBOOK ONLY leads)
> "Hi! Love your work, [a specific real detail from their page]. You've got [reviews] reviews on Google but no
> website, so people searching for [service] in [city] can't find much. I made you a quick preview site, want the link?"

## Follow-up (once, 3–4 days later, same channel)
> "Hi [Business], just checking you saw the website preview I sent: [link]. Happy to change anything. If now's not a
> good time, no problem."

Then stop. Two touches, no more.

## Rules
- Public business contact details only. Never use an owner's personal number, email or home address.
- Write each message for that business. No bulk texting, no bulk email blasts, no auto-dialers.
- Follow your local rules (US: CAN-SPAM for email, TCPA for texts/calls; Canada: CASL; UK/EU: PECR + GDPR).
- If they say no or ask you to stop, stop and note it.
