# Outreach for broken or outdated websites

Lead with what the CUSTOMER sees, in plain words, and offer the proof. The owner almost never knows. Be the helpful
neighbour who noticed, not the alarmist. Only mention problems you verified in a real browser.

## What to say, by problem
| verified problem | say it like this |
|---|---|
| NOT SECURE (http only) | "When I opened your website, Chrome put a 'Not secure' label next to the address. A lot of people back out when they see that." |
| NOT SECURE (expired / invalid certificate) | "Your website is showing a full-page security warning right now, the 'Your connection is not private' screen. Most people won't click past it." |
| DOWN | "I tried to visit your website and it doesn't load at all. The domain doesn't seem to be working." |
| PARKED | "Your web address is showing a 'domain for sale' / parked page instead of your business." |
| ERROR | "Your homepage is showing an error message instead of your site." |
| DATED | "Your site still says © 2014 at the bottom and it's hard to use on a phone, which is where most people will find you." |

## Phone
> "Hi, is this [Business]? Quick heads-up: I was looking at [service] in [city] and found you on Google, [rating]
> stars from [reviews] reviews, nice. When I clicked through to your website, [problem in plain words]. I build and
> fix websites for local businesses. Want me to text or email you a screenshot so you can see it?"

If they ask "how much to fix?": "Some of it might be a quick fix. Let me look properly and I'll tell you honestly."

## Email (only to a business address listed publicly)
Subject: Your website is showing [a security warning / an error / a for-sale page]
> Hi [Business] team,
>
> I came across you on Google Maps ([rating] stars from [reviews] reviews). When I clicked through to [domain],
> [problem in plain words]. Screenshot attached so you can see what customers see.
>
> I fix and rebuild websites for local businesses. If you'd like, I can also show you a preview of a fresh site built
> from your Google listing.
>
> [Name] · [phone]
>
> (Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.)

## Walk-in
Show the problem on your phone: open their site in front of them. Then show the preview you made.

## Follow-up (once, 3–4 days later)
> "Hi [Business], just checking you saw my note about your website [problem]. Happy to help either way."

Then stop. Two touches, no more.

## Rules
- Only problems you verified in a browser, with a screenshot. Never guess, never exaggerate, never say "hacked"
  unless you can see it.
- Don't test anything beyond loading the public homepage like a normal visitor.
- Public business contact details only; one message per business; follow your local anti-spam rules; stop on a no.
