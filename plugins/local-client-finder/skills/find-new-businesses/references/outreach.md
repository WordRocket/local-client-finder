# Outreach for new businesses

Congratulate first. Offer ONE concrete, useful thing. Keep it short. New owners are busy and get a flood of sales
calls in their first months: be the one who already did something for them.

## Walk-in (best for storefronts, restaurants, salons)
Go at a quiet hour. Buy something small if it's that kind of place.
> "Congrats on opening! I build websites for local businesses and noticed you don't have one yet, so people who
> search for you can't find much. I made a quick preview of what yours could look like. Want to see it?"
Show it on your phone.

## Phone
> "Hi, is this [Business]? Congratulations on opening on [street / area]. I help new local businesses get found on
> Google: website and Google profile. I noticed you don't have a website yet, so I put together a free preview. Can I
> text or email you the link?"

## Email / DM (business email or their business page only)
Subject: Congrats on opening, [Business]
> Hi [Business] team,
>
> Congratulations on opening in [area]! I noticed you don't have a website [or Google listing] yet, which is where most
> people look before they visit.
>
> I made you a free preview of a site: [preview link]. If you like it, I can set it up properly, along with your
> Google Business Profile so you show up on Maps.
>
> [Name] · [phone]
>
> (Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.)

## If they have no Google Business Profile yet
Lead with that: "Right now you don't show up on Google Maps at all. I can help you set that up this week." It's the
fastest win for a new business, and it's what RankSite builds the site from.

## Follow-up (once, about a week later)
> "Hi [Business], hope the first weeks are going well! Just checking you saw the website preview: [link]."

Then stop.

## Rules
- Public business channels only. Never a home address, a registered agent, or an owner's personal number/email.
- One message per business, written for them. No bulk texting or email blasts.
- Follow your local anti-spam rules (CAN-SPAM / TCPA in the US, CASL in Canada, PECR / GDPR in the UK and EU).
- If they say no, stop.
