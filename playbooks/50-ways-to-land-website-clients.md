# 50 ways to land website clients, ranked S tier to C tier

Run them with Claude + the free Local Client Finder skills + RankSite. S tier = do these first (highest return for the
time). C tier = still works, but lower return. Results vary: your niche, town and follow-up decide yours.

## S tier: start here
1. **Build the site first, then pitch.** A preview of their new site beats any sales email. *Claude: "Build a preview site for this business: [Google Maps link]"*
2. **Great reviews, no website.** 4.5★+ with 20+ reviews and no Website button on Google Maps. *Claude: "Find me clients: [trade] in [city]"*
3. **Broken websites.** Dead domains, "Not secure" warnings, error pages: show them the screenshot. *Claude: "Find [trade] in [city] with broken websites"*
4. **Brand-new businesses.** Cities publish new business licences: be first in the door. *Claude: "Who just opened in [city] and has no website?"*
5. **Call, don't email.** Phone them with their name, rating and review count in the first sentence.
6. **Walk in with the preview on your phone** (storefronts, restaurants, salons).
7. **Lead with their own reviews.** "Your customers say you're the best in town. Google doesn't show it."
8. **Sell the monthly, not just the site.** Hosting + SEO pages every month is where the income compounds.
9. **Show who's beating them.** Search "[service] [city]" with them and point at the competitor on top.
10. **A page for every service in every town.** It's the clearest SEO promise a local owner understands. *Claude: "Plan the SEO for this site"*

## A tier: strong, do them next
11. **Facebook-only businesses.** Their "website" is a Facebook page: easy upgrade.
12. **Outdated sites.** © 2016–2020 in the footer, not mobile-friendly, no booking button.
13. **High-ticket trades.** Roofing, HVAC, remodeling, foundation repair: one job pays for a year of your fees.
14. **Businesses paying for ads that land on a weak page.** Check Google's Ads Transparency Center.
15. **Growth signals.** Hiring, a second location, new services: budget is already moving.
16. **The smaller towns around your city.** More businesses without sites, less competition.
17. **Three follow-ups.** Day 3, day 7, day 14. Most yeses come after the first no-reply.
18. **A 60-second video audit** of their current site, sent with the preview.
19. **Ask every client for one referral** the day their site goes live.
20. **Partner with accountants, bookkeepers and insurance brokers** who serve the same owners.
21. **Niche down.** One trade, one region: become "the website person for roofers".
22. **Fix their Google Business Profile first** as a free win; it opens the door to the paid work.
23. **Sell a review-request system** as an add-on.
24. **Three packages, anchored high.** Most owners pick the middle one.
25. **A case study within 30 days** of your first client (with their permission).

## B tier: good extras
26. **Local Facebook groups:** answer questions, never spam.
27. **Chamber of commerce and networking groups (BNI and similar).**
28. **Personal cold email with the preview link** (business address + opt-out in every email).
29. **LinkedIn messages to practice managers** (clinics, law firms, dental offices).
30. **Instagram DMs** to businesses that post often but have no website.
31. **Yelp "Hot and New"** listings in your city.
32. **"Now open" news** in local papers and city blogs.
33. **A free "can ChatGPT find you?" check** as the opener.
34. **White-label for small agencies** that don't want to build sites.
35. **Nextdoor business pages** in the neighbourhoods you serve.
36. **Trade association member directories.**
37. **Sponsor one local event** your niche attends.
38. **A short workshop** at the library or chamber: "Get found on Google in 2027".
39. **Re-contact last year's "not now" leads** every quarter.
40. **Bundle with a photographer or signage shop** that serves the same clients.

## C tier: works, lower return
41. Postcards or door hangers with a QR code to the preview.
42. Classified ads (Craigslist, Kijiji) for your services.
43. Freelance marketplaces for your first reviews only.
44. Generic social posts saying "I build websites".
45. Mass LinkedIn connection requests without a message.
46. Cold email without a preview or a real reason to write.
47. Leading with a discount.
48. Paid ads for your own agency before you have case studies.
49. Directory listings for your agency.
50. Waiting for word of mouth alone.
