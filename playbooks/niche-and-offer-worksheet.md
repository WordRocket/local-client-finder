# Pick a niche and an offer that sells itself

A 10-minute worksheet for anyone selling to local businesses: websites, SEO, Google profiles, reviews, social, ads or
automations. Do this before you look for a single lead: it decides who you call, what you say and what you charge.

---

## 1. Score 3 to 5 trades in your town

Open Google Maps, search `[trade] in [your town]` for each trade you're considering, and score it 1 to 5 on each line.

| | What it means | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Job value** | What one customer is worth to them (one job × how often they come back) | under $150 | $1,000+ |
| **Gaps** | How many listings show the problem YOU fix (see "What a gap looks like" below) | almost none | 1 in 5 or more |
| **Busy** | How many have 20+ reviews at 4.5★ or better (busy, trusted, missing the obvious) | a few | most of them |
| **Reachable** | Phone listed, a storefront you can walk into, owner-run | call centres, chains | owner answers |
| **Your edge** | You know the trade, know someone in it, or actually like it | none | strong |

| Trade | Job value | Gaps | Busy | Reachable | Your edge | Total /25 |

| example: pressure washing, Tulsa | 4 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 2 | 18 |

### What a gap looks like, for what you sell
| You sell | Count the listings that… |
|---|---|
| **Websites** | have no Website button, or a site that's broken, "Not secure" or clearly old (© 2021 or earlier) |
| **Local SEO** | have good reviews but don't show in the top 3 map results for "[trade] in [town]", or one page for every service |
| **Google profile + reviews** | have fewer than 20 reviews, no review in the last 3 months, few photos, or reviews with no owner reply |
| **Social media** | have no Facebook/Instagram linked, or the last post is 3+ months old |
| **Ads** | sit under competitors' Sponsored results or Local Services Ads for their own trade and town, with none of their own |
| **Automations** (booking, missed-call text-back, chat) | say "call for a quote" with no online booking, or have reviews about "no answer" or "never called back" |

**18 or more: start there.** Pick ONE trade and ONE area for your first 30 days. "The website person for pressure
washers in Tulsa" is easier to say, easier to trust and easier to price than "I do websites". Same for "the reviews
person for dentists in Mesa" vs "I do marketing".

For scale, a real search (Google Maps, Oct 2026): "pressure washing in Tulsa OK" had 72 listings, 10 without a
Website button. Busy trades in the suburbs and smaller towns next to a city usually have more gaps than downtown.

Good trades to score first: pressure washing, landscaping and lawn care, tree service, junk removal, painting, fencing,
decks, handyman, cleaning, mobile detailing, pool service, pest control. Higher-ticket (bigger fees, harder sell):
roofing, HVAC, remodeling, clinics, dental, law.

## 2. Sell the outcome, not the website

Owners don't want "a website", "SEO" or "content". They want the phone to ring. Fill in your one-line offer:

> I help **[trade]** in **[area]** get more **[calls / booked jobs]** from Google, with a site that shows every service
> and every town they cover, **[plus the monthly thing you do]**.

Examples:
- Websites: *"I help pressure washers around Tulsa get more booked jobs from Google, with a site that shows every service
  and every town they cover, plus a page a month and a review system that keeps them on top."*
- Reviews: *"I help dentists in Mesa get a steady stream of fresh Google reviews, so new patients pick them over the
  practice down the street."*
- Automations: *"I make sure every missed call at your shop gets a text back in a minute, so the job doesn't go to the
  next company on the list."*

**Websites open the door.** A free preview of their own site is the easiest "yes" to get, whatever you sell after it:
SEO, reviews and the monthly plan are what they stay for.

## 3. Three packages (you set the prices)

Most owners pick the middle option, so make the middle one the one you want to sell.

| | Starter | Get Calls (recommended) | Grow |
|---|---|---|---|
| What they get | Site live: home, services, contact, call button | Starter + a page for every service and town + Google Business Profile set up properly | Get Calls + monthly SEO pages, a review-request system, a one-page monthly report |
| Price shape | one-time | one-time + small monthly (hosting) | monthly |

Selling something else? Keep the same shape: a one-time setup, a recommended monthly plan, a bigger monthly plan. For
reviews, that's the review-request setup / monthly requests + replies to every review / that plus profile posts and
photos. For automations: one missed-call text-back / text-back + online booking / that plus follow-ups and reminders.

**What the market charges, for reference** (full table in the free "2026 price guide"): freelancers' most common website
bands are $1,000–$2,500 and $2,500–$5,000; the most common local SEO retainer is $501–$1,000 a month (local SEO averages
$1,557, Ahrefs); Google profile management runs about $125–$600 per location a month; managed reviews $500–$1,000 a
month for a small business. You don't have to start there: lower prices while you build proof are fine, as long as you
raise them once you have results.

Payment terms that protect you: 50% up front, 50% at launch; monthly billed in advance; 30 days' notice to cancel.

## 4. The founding-client deal (your first 2 or 3)

No portfolio yet? Offer your first 2 or 3 clients a lower price in exchange for three things, in writing:
1. A testimonial you can quote (in their words, approved by them)
2. Permission to show their before and after as a case study
3. One introduction to another business owner they know

Ask for an honest Google review the same way you'd ask any client, but never as part of the deal: Google doesn't allow
reviews in exchange for discounts or anything else.

Make it honest and time-limited: *"I'm taking 3 founding clients in [area] this month at [price]."* Only say it if it's
true.

## 5. Your one-pager (fill it in, then use it everywhere)

- **Who I help:** [trade] in [area]
- **What I see:** [their customers search Google and find a competitor / a broken page / nothing]
- **What I do:** [your one-line offer from section 2]
- **Packages:** Starter [$] · Get Calls [$ + $/mo] · Grow [$/mo]
- **Proof:** [a preview site you built for them, or your first case study]
- **Next step:** [call / text / walk-in], [your name], [phone], [business address]
