# What to charge local businesses in 2026: a price guide by service

Real ranges from surveys, marketplaces and published price lists, checked in November 2026. Use them to build
packages you can say with a straight face, not to copy someone else's price. All figures in US dollars.

## How to set YOUR price in 4 steps

1. **Start from the owner's math.** One job worth $450, half of callers book: a $300/month plan pays for itself with
   less than 2 extra calls a month. If you can't show that math with their numbers, the price is too high for now.
2. **Pick your three packages** (worksheet section 3): a one-time setup, a recommended monthly plan, a bigger monthly
   plan. Most owners pick the middle one, so make it the one you want to sell.
3. **Your first 2–3 clients: a founding price.** Lower, time-limited and honest ("I'm taking 3 founding clients in
   [area] this month"), in exchange for a testimonial, a case study and an introduction.
4. **Raise it after proof.** In a 2026 survey of 622 WordPress agencies, about 1 in 3 was consistently profitable under
   $5,000 per project and nearly 1 in 2 at $5,000+ (The Admin Bar). Results first, then the price.

## Payment terms that protect you

- **Websites:** a deposit before you start. Most web designers ask 25–50% up front; under $5,000, 50% is typical
  (Contra 2025). The rest at launch, before the site goes live on their domain.
- **Monthly plans:** billed in advance, every month. Reviews and SEO contracts often run 3 to 6 months for small
  businesses; month-to-month is easier to sell when you're new.
- **Ads:** the client pays the ad platform directly with their own card. You charge only your fee.
- **Notice:** 30 days to cancel, in writing.

---

## Two rules that keep you out of trouble

- **Reviews:** since October 21, 2024, the FTC bans buying or selling fake reviews, paying for positive or negative
  reviews, and some review-suppression tricks. Google's policy also bans "review gating": asking only happy customers
  for a review. Any review service you sell must ask every customer.
- **Never promise rankings or a number of calls.** Promise the work and the report; let the numbers speak.

## How to read these ranges

The same service can cost 10× more from one provider than another. Three things drive it:
- **Location:** US/Canada agencies charge far more than the global marketplace medians; 22% of US/Canada SEO agencies
  charge over $2,000 a month vs 9% in Europe (SE Ranking 2024). Big-city agencies quote more than small-town
  freelancers. Canada-only data is thin: Canadian agencies sit in the same $100–$149/hr band on Clutch.
- **Experience:** SEOs with 2+ years charge about 33% more (Ahrefs); social media freelancers with 7+ years bill about
  twice the hourly rate of beginners (HeyOrca).
- **Scope:** pages, who writes the copy, platforms, posts per week, ad budget, number of locations.

Surveys are self-reported; Clutch averages lean on big projects; guides from companies that sell the service have a
stake in their numbers; some data is older (2021–2024). Your market will vary.
