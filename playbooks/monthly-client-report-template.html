The one-page monthly client report

In vcita's 2025 survey of 500 US small businesses, the #1 reason owners dropped their marketing agency was no return on investment (60%). Usually that means they couldn't see what they were paying for. This page makes the work visible in 10 minutes a month. Send it in the first week of every month, every month, even a slow one.

How to fill it in without getting it wrong

  • Pick 4–6 numbers and keep the same ones every month, so the trend means something. Don't swap in a better-looking number when one dips.
  • Compare the same length of time. Month vs last month, and once you have a year of data, vs the same month last year (seasonal trades swing a lot).
  • If a number went down, say so first and give the likely reason: season, a holiday, a competitor's new ads, a Google change. Hiding it is how trust ends.
  • Never claim credit you can't show. "Calls went up 18%" is a fact; "my work brought you 18% more calls" usually isn't provable. Say what you did and what changed.
  • Leads beat traffic. Owners care about calls, bookings and forms. Put those first, impressions last or not at all.
  • Note anything unusual: a week the profile was suspended, a site outage, a price change, a big job that skews the month.

The 3-line email to send it with

Subject: [Business] · [Month] report

Hi [Name], your [Month] report is attached. The short version: [headline]. Next month I'm focusing on [#1 from the list]. Anything you'd like me to look at?
