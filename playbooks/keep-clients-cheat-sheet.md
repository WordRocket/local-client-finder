# Keep clients paying (and sending you friends)

Landing a client is half the job. The monthly fee is what makes this a business, and a happy client is your best source
of the next one.

---

## Why small businesses drop their marketing person
In a vcita survey of small businesses that outsourced their marketing, the top reason for leaving was disappointing
results (68%). Practitioners add two more: feeling like they have to chase you for small things, and budget cuts in a
slow season. So: make the work visible, answer fast, and give them options before they leave.

---

## What the monthly fee should cover (and why it's worth it)
- **New pages** for the services and towns they want more of.
- **The Google Business Profile:** weekly posts, fresh photos, services and hours up to date.
- **Reviews, every month.** BrightLocal's 2026 survey: 47% of consumers won't use a business with fewer than 20 reviews,
  and 74% look for reviews from the last three months. A steady trickle of new reviews is one of the easiest wins you
  can deliver.
- **Being findable in AI answers.** 45% of consumers used ChatGPT or other AI tools for local recommendations in 2026, up
  from 6% a year earlier (BrightLocal). Consistent business info, real FAQs and clear service pages help.
- **Fixes** within a day: a broken form, wrong hours, a new phone number.
- **A one-page report.**

## The one-page monthly report
- One honest headline ("12 new pages live, and Google showed you more often than last month").
- 4–5 real numbers vs last month, each with its source: pages published, profile calls, website clicks, form leads, new
  reviews. If a number went down, say so and say why.
- What you did this month, and the 3 things you'll do next month.
- One suggestion that would help them most.
Send it in the first week of every month with a 3-line message.

---

## The 90-day review (15 minutes)
1. What changed since launch (the numbers).
2. What they want more of now.
3. "Anything you'd change about how we work?"
Then the next 90 days in 3 bullet points.

## Ask for referrals at the 3 happiest moments
1. **Launch week:** "So glad you like it. Who else do you know who'd want this?"
2. **Day 30:** after the first calls or leads come in.
3. **After a good report:** "Your profile calls are up this month. If a friend's business could use the same, I'd love an
   intro."
Make it easy with a text they can forward: *"I used [your name] for our website, worth talking to: [phone]."*

## When they want to cancel
Thank them, ask what's behind it, then offer the option that fits: a pause for 1–3 months, the smaller package, or one
clear goal for the next 60 days. If they still want to go, hand everything over cleanly (domain, content, access) and
leave on good terms. A good goodbye is a future referral.
